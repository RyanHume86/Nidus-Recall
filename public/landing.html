Nidus Recall remember everything

Spaced repetition for serious learners

Built for postgraduate medicine and science. Nidus Recall uses the FSRS-5 algorithm to schedule your reviews at the optimal moment, so you retain more with less time at the desk.

Draw masks over diagrams and anatomical images. Each region becomes an independent card.
Sleep-aware reminders
Optional bedtime window shifts to review-only mode, aligned with sleep consolidation evidence (Diekelmann and Born, 2010).
Anki import
Import .apkg decks directly. Basic, cloze, and image occlusion card types are converted automatically.
Offline PWA
Install on any device. Reviews queue locally when offline and sync when connection returns.
