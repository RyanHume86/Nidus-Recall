# Privacy Policy

**Nidus Recall** — Version 1.0 · Effective 1 May 2026

---

## Who We Are

Nidus Recall is a spaced-repetition learning tool designed for postgraduate learners in health professions. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information, and how you can exercise your rights under the **Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA)**.

---

## What Information We Collect

### Information you provide directly

- **Name** — your first name, which you may optionally provide for personalised greetings.
- **Flashcard content** — the questions, answers, notes, anchors, and tags you create. This content belongs to you.
- **Study sessions** — aggregated session statistics (number of cards reviewed, failed, and added per session). No session content is logged.
- **Scheduling preferences** — study caps, sleep window settings, and retention targets you configure.

### Information collected automatically

- **Account credentials** — your email address and authentication token, managed by our infrastructure provider Base44.
- **Usage data** — card review ratings (again/hard/good/easy) and scheduling state (stability, difficulty, interval), used to drive the spaced-repetition algorithm.
- **Device type** — inferred from your browser user-agent for compatibility purposes only; not stored.

---

## Why We Process Your Information

We process your personal information to:

1. Provide the Nidus Recall spaced-repetition service.
2. Personalise your review schedule using the FSRS-5 algorithm.
3. Allow you to export and delete your own data.
4. Diagnose technical faults where you report them.

We do **not** process your information for advertising, profiling, or sale to third parties.

---

## Third-Party Processors

| Processor | Purpose | Location |
|-----------|---------|----------|
| **Base44** | Application backend, data storage, authentication | United States (AWS) |

Base44 processes data under a Data Processing Agreement on our behalf. Your data may be stored on servers outside South Africa. We rely on Standard Contractual Clauses as the cross-border transfer mechanism in terms of POPIA Section 72.

---

## How Long We Retain Your Data

| Data type | Retention period |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Flashcards, decks, session logs | Until you delete them or request account deletion |
| Account record | Up to 30 days after account deletion request |
| Backups | Up to 30 days after account deletion request |

---

## Your Rights Under POPIA (Sections 23–25)

As a data subject, you have the right to:

- **Access** (Section 23) — request confirmation of whether we hold information about you, and a description of that information.
- **Correction or deletion** (Section 24) — request that inaccurate, irrelevant, excessive, out-of-date, incomplete, misleading, or unlawfully obtained information be corrected or deleted.
- **Objection** (Section 25) — object to the processing of your personal information on reasonable grounds.

To exercise these rights, use the **Export my data** and **Delete my account** functions in Settings → Privacy, or email us at **privacy@nidusrecall.app**.
